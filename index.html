Bogata, Colombia Partnership

As believers, we are called to make disciples of all nations! We find this command in Scripture in Matthew 28:18.

In Bogata, there are 8 million pepole! of these, there are over 1 million college students. Many of these have a Catholic background, but now no longer associate with Christianity.

Village Church has started a partnership with a church in Bogata named Iglesia Bautista Renacer. This is a likeminded pillar network church.
